GRAVITY ANCHOR Press & Hold mouse/touch/spacebar near a node planet to latch on and swing in orbit.

03
LIGHT PULSES & COMBOS Collect glowing yellow gems. Skim close to asteroids without crashing to multiply your score combo!
04
LEVELS & POWER-UPS Climb higher to level up: everything gets faster and more crowded, but each level multiplies your points. The background glow shifts colour with every new level. Grab a golden 2X orb for double points, and a pink ♥ for an extra life.
05
GATE PLANETS A gravity wall blocks the end of every level. Fly or swing into the ringed planet beneath it to dive inside a 30-second arcade challenge. Slide your thumb anywhere (phone) or move the mouse (PC) to steer — firing is automatic, tap/click to launch. Survive or win to break the wall and reach the next level; fail and you lose a life.

PLASMA OVERCHARGE Keep holding while you orbit to fill the plasma ring around your orb. Release once it glows to fire a sonic burst: a huge speed boost, brief invulnerability, and small asteroids in your path get smashed. A fully charged burst smashes everything.
